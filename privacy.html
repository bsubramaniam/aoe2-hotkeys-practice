Last updated: 24 July 2026

Privacy

Hotkey files and custom drill JSON files you choose are processed entirely in your browser. This application does not upload those files to a server.

Only custom drills are stored persistently by the application, using your browser's local storage. Uploaded hotkey files, active drill progress, difficulty choices, and other settings are kept only for the current page session and are not persisted by the application.

Custom drills remain in that browser until you delete them through the application or clear this site's data in your browser. Session-only data is discarded when the page session ends.

A browser-local drill ID is used in the page URL when you select or edit a drill. For a custom drill, this ID is derived from its name. Cloudflare may therefore process that ID as part of the requested URL. Do not include sensitive personal information in a drill name.

The beta has no accounts or advertising. The application does not set advertising or analytics cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate page visits and website performance. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage for analytics and does not fingerprint individuals for analytics.

Cloudflare also hosts and protects the website and may process ordinary technical request data, including IP addresses and requested URLs, as needed to deliver and secure the site. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies when required. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

The application does not transmit the contents of your hotkey files or custom drill files, your resolved mappings, individual hotkey inputs, or drill results to us or other users.

Contact us about privacy.
